FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions answered.

How do I get started?

Fill in the form above or call us directly. We'll schedule an initial consultation — usually 30–60 minutes — to understand your space, requirements and budget before recommending next steps.

Do you work outside Kolkata?

Yes. We have delivered projects across India. For pan-India projects, we coordinate remotely and schedule site visits at key milestones. Share your location and we'll discuss how we can make it work.

What is the typical project timeline?

A 2–3 BHK home interior typically takes 3–5 months from design sign-off to handover, depending on scope and site readiness. We'll give you a realistic schedule at the brief stage.

Is there a design consultation fee?

The initial consultation is complimentary. Design fees vary by project scope and are discussed transparently at the proposal stage — no hidden charges.
